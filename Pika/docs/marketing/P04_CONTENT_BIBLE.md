## 1. Product Identity

Pika is a B2B SaaS platform engineered to unify commercial intelligence with controlled omnichannel execution. 

### The Core Axiom
- **Turkish (TR):**  
  > "Pika’nın başlangıç noktası mesaj göndermek değil, anlamaktır."  
  *(Status: APPROVED_COPY | Source: PRODUCT_OWNER_APPROVED_CANON)*
- **English (EN):**  
  > "Pika’s starting point is not merely sending messages; it is understanding customer, product, and transaction context."  
  *(Status: APPROVED_COPY | Source: PRODUCT_OWNER_APPROVED_CANON)*

### Architectural Identity: What Pika Is NOT
To maintain strategic positioning and prevent dilution into commoditized software categories, Pika must never be defined or marketed primarily as:
1. **Bulk Messaging Software:** Pika does not exist to blast unsegmented SMS or broadcast email lists.
2. **Email Marketing Tool:** Email is one execution pipe among several, not the platform's core identity.
3. **WhatsApp Marketing Tool:** WhatsApp is an authenticated API delivery channel, not a standalone chatbot.
4. **CRM Replacement:** Pika does not replace transactional sales pipelines, contact ticketing, or operational ERPs; it enriches customer data with predictive intelligence.
5. **Generic CDP (Customer Data Platform):** Pika is not a passive data lake; it pairs identity data directly with daily commercial opportunity generation.
6. **AI Chatbot / Conversational Bot:** Pika does not offer autonomous customer-facing dialogue agents.
7. **Generic Marketing Automation:** Pika is distinguished by its twin analytical engines (Customer Intelligence and Product Intelligence) and daily actionable opportunity discovery.

### The 7-Step Strategic Story
Pika's narrative must always flow from foundation to business impact, never starting backwards with communication channels:

1. **DATA:** Pika begins with raw customer, product, and transaction context (POS, e-commerce, ERP).
2. **UNDERSTANDING:** Customer Intelligence and Product Intelligence turn raw commercial records into usable behavioural context.
3. **OPPORTUNITY:** Pika identifies where a meaningful customer/product opportunity exists (consumption rhythms, basket affinity, churn prevention).
4. **DECISION:** Pika determines the optimal audience segment, timing window, and channel context.
5. **ACTION:** Audience Manager, Campaign Manager, Journey Manager, and Content Studio turn the decision into controlled execution.
6. **CHANNELS:** Execution is delivered across confirmed, governed channels: Email, SMS, and WhatsApp. *(Push notifications are unconfirmed and excluded from marketing).*
7. **MEASUREMENT:** Analytics, BI Kokpit, and revenue attribution close the loop by tracking delivery status machines, customer engagement, and transaction outcomes attributed to campaigns.

## 6. Platform Value Chain

The platform value chain enforces the strict architectural hierarchy of Pika. Future product views and diagrams must reflect this exact flow:

$$\text{DATA} \longrightarrow \text{CUSTOMER \& PRODUCT INTELLIGENCE} \longrightarrow \text{OPPORTUNITY / DECISION} \longrightarrow \text{AUDIENCE / CAMPAIGN / JOURNEY} \longrightarrow \text{EMAIL / SMS / WHATSAPP} \longrightarrow \text{MEASUREMENT \& LEARNING}$$

```
[ INGESTION & DATA LAYER ]
  • Transactions, POS, E-Commerce, CSV/Excel Ingestion
  • Contact lists, attributes, consent tokens
       │
       ▼
[ INTELLIGENCE ENGINES ]
  • Customer Intelligence: Customer Value Score (CVS), Purchasing Cycle (80%-120%), Churn Risk
  • Product Intelligence: Need Groups, Product Roles (Traffic Driver, Basket Booster, Margin)
       │
       ▼
[ OPPORTUNITY & DECISION LAYER ]
  • Günün Fırsatları (Daily Opportunities / CCE)
  • Replenishment alerts, Market Basket cross-sells, Win-back cohorts
       │
       ▼
[ ENGAGEMENT & ORCHESTRATION ]
  • Audience Manager: Dynamic rule-based customer segments
  • Campaign Manager: Multi-channel broadcast scheduling & attribution
  • Journey Manager: Multi-step event-driven automation canvas
  • Content Studio: Drag-and-drop template design & block library
  • Pika Pilot: AI-assisted campaign brief & template generation
       │
       ▼
[ EXECUTION CHANNELS ]
  • Email (HTML drag-and-drop templates, transactional & marketing)
  • SMS (Rate-limited dispatcher, gateway delivery)
  • WhatsApp (Official WhatsApp Business API approved templates)
       │
       ▼
[ MEASUREMENT & GOVERNANCE ]
  • BI Kokpit: Delivery telemetry, bounce tracking, engagement rates
  • Revenue Attribution: Sales turnover mapped to campaign touchpoints
  • Consent & Compliance: IYS verification, KVKK consent token audit
```

*(Note: Push notifications are CONTRADICTORY / UNCONFIRMED in product governance and are omitted from the active value chain).*

## 7. AI Positioning

### The AI Conceptual Boundary
Pika maintains a strict boundary between deterministic intelligence and assistive AI. Generative AI is an accelerator, never the source of business truth or an autonomous agent:

- **Turkish (TR):**  
  > Pika AI, müşteriyi veya fırsatı hayal ederek oluşturmaz. Pika'nın hesapladığı müşteri, ürün ve fırsat bağlamını kullanarak kampanya hazırlığını ve içerik üretimini hızlandırır; son karar kullanıcıda kalır.  
  *(Status: APPROVED_COPY | Source: PRODUCT_OWNER_APPROVED_CANON)*
- **English (EN):**  
  > Pika AI does not invent the customer or the opportunity. It uses customer, product and opportunity context calculated by Pika to accelerate campaign preparation and content creation, while the final decision remains with the user.  
  *(Status: APPROVED_COPY | Source: PRODUCT_OWNER_APPROVED_CANON)*

### AI Governance Architecture
$$\text{DETERMINISTIC INTELLIGENCE} \longrightarrow \text{AI EXPLANATION / CREATION} \longrightarrow \text{HUMAN CONTROL} \longrightarrow \text{EXECUTION}$$

1. **Deterministic Foundation:** Customer Value Scores, cycle percentages, product affinities, and segment inclusions are computed by mathematical algorithms, not generative LLMs.
2. **AI Assistance (Pika Pilot):** Translates campaign briefs into template designs, recommends subject lines, and suggests audience rules based strictly on provided context.
3. **Mandatory Human Control:** No campaign or journey step can be triggered autonomously by AI. Every action requires explicit marketer confirmation. *(Claim: CLM-017 | Level A CODE_VERIFIED)*
4. **Data Privacy & AI Boundary:** Internal engineering documentation specifies that personal identifying information (TCKN, raw phone numbers, full names) is omitted from LLM prompt tokens, using only anonymized behavioral parameters (Level B DOCUMENTED | Claim: CLM-018). An absolute public marketing guarantee of "Zero-PII" requires explicit confirmation before being published as an unconditioned claim (Status: NEEDS_PRODUCT_CONFIRMATION).

## 9. Voice & Tone

Pika speaks with the calm, precise, and operationally serious tone of a controlled B2B platform.

### The 6 Voice Attributes
1. **PRECISE:** Use exact, unambiguous terminology (e.g., "İYS izin doğrulaması", not "yasal işler").
2. **SPECIFIC:** State exact software mechanisms rather than vague abstractions (e.g., "Satın alma döngüsü %80-%120 penceresi", not "akıllı zamanlama").
3. **CALM:** Avoid exclamation marks, urgent sales pitches, artificial FOMO, and breathless marketing claims.
4. **COMMERCIAL:** Speak to retail metrics that matter to commercial leaders (ciro atfı, sepet büyüklüğü, tekrar satın alma sıklığı, kayıp riski).
5. **PRODUCT-LED:** Let real platform interfaces, workflows, and data structures demonstrate capability.
6. **EVIDENCE-AWARE:** Never claim capabilities, integrations, or metrics that cannot be verified in the codebase or authoritative documentation.

### Vocabulary Guardrails
- **Prohibited Buzzwords:** "Revolutionary", "game-changing", "world-class", "best-in-class", "enterprise-grade" (unsubstantiated), "mission-critical" (unsubstantiated), "magic", "seamless", "disruptive", "effortless", "all-in-one" (tümleşik/omnichannel tercih edilmelidir).
- **The 5 "What" Anchors:** Every product section must clearly answer:
  1. *What Pika knows* (Veri ve bağlam)
  2. *What Pika finds* (Ticari fırsat)
  3. *What the user can do* (Operasyonel yetenek)
  4. *What control exists* (İzin, kural, insan onayı)
  5. *What can be measured* (İlişkilendirilen ciro ve dönüşüm etkisi)

## 18. Copy Ownership States

To ensure transparency between automated agent work and human editorial prerogative, every content specification in Pika Web 2.0 uses one of these 5 explicit states:

1. **`APPROVED_COPY`:**
   Exact wording has been validated against product truth and approved directly by product governance. It may be reused verbatim in marketing views, SEO tags, and documentation.
2. **`APPROVED_CONCEPT`:**
   The semantic meaning, value proposition, and technical scope are approved. The final polished marketing phrasing remains the responsibility of the human content owner.
3. **`NEEDS_CONTENT_OWNER`:**
   Approved source material does not contain final copy or specific section text. Codex and automated agents must not invent creative prose; this tag explicitly marks the gap for human authoring.
4. **`NEEDS_PRODUCT_CONFIRMATION`:**
   Product architecture, feature status, or legal boundaries are unresolved (e.g., Push timeline, SOC attestation, company headquarters). Copy cannot be written until product leadership confirms the fact.
5. **`DO_NOT_MARKET`:**
   The capability, price point, or claim is refuted, outdated, or legally unverified. It is strictly prohibited from appearing in any public view or metadata.

## 22. Forbidden Language

The following claims, phrases, and marketing anti-patterns are **strictly forbidden** across all Pika Web 2.0 copy:

1. **Autonomous Marketing Claims:**
   - "Yapay zekâ sizin yerinize tüm kampanyaları yönetir ve gönderir."
   - "Fully automated self-driving marketing."
   - "Autonomous marketing robot."
2. **Guaranteed Financial / Metric Uplifts:**
   - "Garantili ciro artışı" / "Guaranteed revenue growth"
   - "Dönüşümde kesin %40 artış" / "Guaranteed 14.2x ROAS"
   - "Garantili teslimat oranı" / "100% inbox placement guarantee"
3. **Absolute Legal Guarantees:**
   - "%100 KVKK ve ceza muafiyeti garantisi"
   - "Zero-risk regulatory immunity"
4. **Unsubstantiated Security Standards:**
   - "Askeri düzeyde güvenlik" / "Military-grade encryption"
   - "SOC-2 Type II Certified" *(unless official audit report is delivered)*
   - "SAML 2.0 / Okta SSO Ready" *(until middleware is merged)*
5. **Conversational WhatsApp Claims:**
   - "WhatsApp chatbotu" / "AI conversational customer support"
   - "Müşterilerinizle iki yönlü sohbet eden akıllı bot"
6. **Push as Live Channel or Unconfirmed Capability:**
   - "4 yerel kanal: E-posta, SMS, WhatsApp ve Push" *(Push bildirimleri ürün sahibi tarafından teyit edilmeden canlı veya roadmap kanal olarak sunulamaz; unconfirmed statüsündedir)*
7. **Exaggerated Speed / Throughput:**
   - "Milisaniyeler içinde milyonlarca mesaj işleme"
   - "Unlimited real-time scale"
8. **Fixed Pricing & Package Claims:**
   - "Ayda ₺9.900'den başlayan fiyatlarla"
   - "Ücretsiz deneme sürümü" / "Kredi kartı gerekmez"
